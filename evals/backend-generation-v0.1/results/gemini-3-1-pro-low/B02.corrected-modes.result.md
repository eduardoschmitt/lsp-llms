## Hypothesis

Open modes must match the operation family: `Gravarnl` mode for `Gravarnl`
writes and `Lernl` mode for `Lernl` reads, as demonstrated both by official
Senior article 6602 (`Abrir(vNomeArq, Gravarnl)` + `Gravarnl(...)`,
`Abrir(vNomeArq, Lernl)` + `Lernl(...)`) and by the corpus temp-file example.
The generated rule mixed `Gravar` mode with `Gravarnl` calls (and `Ler` mode
with a `Lernl` call).
